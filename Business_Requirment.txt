PROJECT: Spotify Listening Analysis (Albums, Artists, Tracks)
TOOL: Power BI
PURPOSE: To understand listening behaviour and identify patterns across albums, artists, and tracks.

------------------------------------------------------------
1. ALBUMS ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

• Total Albums Played Over Time  
  - Track monthly and yearly album listening trends.

• Number of Albums Listened by Year  
  - Identify annual listening habits.
  - Display minimum and maximum albums listened per year.

• Albums Played on Weekday & Weekend  
  - Compare listening behaviour between weekdays and weekends.

• Top 5 Albums  
  - Identify the most played albums based on listening frequency.

• Latest Year vs Previous Year Comparison  
  - LY (Latest Year) vs PY (Previous Year) trends.
  - YoY (Year-over-Year) growth rate.

------------------------------------------------------------
2. ARTISTS ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

• Total Artists Played Over Time  
  - Track artist listening trends across months and years.

• Number of Artists Listened by Year  
  - Identify annual listening habits and artist diversity.
  - Highlight Minimum and Maximum values.

• Artists Played on Weekday & Weekend  
  - Identify listening differences between weekdays and weekends.

• Top 5 Artists  
  - Identify most played artists.

• Latest Year vs Previous Year Comparison  
  - LY vs PY artist trends.
  - YoY growth analysis.

------------------------------------------------------------
3. TRACKS ANALYSIS
------------------------------------------------------------

• Total Tracks Played Over Time  
  - Monitor monthly and yearly track listening trends.

• Number of Tracks Listened by Year  
  - Identify track diversity across years.
  - Display Min and Max tracks per year.

• Tracks Played on Weekday & Weekend  
  - Pattern of weekday vs weekend listening.

• Top 5 Tracks  
  - Identify the most played tracks.

• Latest Year vs Previous Year Comparison  
  - LY vs PY trends.
  - YoY (Year-over-Year) growth for tracks.
